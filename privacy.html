Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 2026

This Privacy Policy explains what information BLOOD REIGN ("we", "us") collects when you use our platform, how we use it, and the choices you have. By using the app, you agree to this policy.

1. Information We Collect

  • Account info: username, email (if you sign up), Free Fire UID.
  • Tournament & team data: registrations, team rosters, scrims, tryout applications.
  • Coins & transactions: your coin balance and history of how you earned/spent coins.
  • Orders: product ordered and delivery address, when you buy from the Shop.
  • Usage data: a lightweight "last active" timestamp used only to show an online/offline status — no browsing history or location is tracked.

2. How We Use Your Information

  • To run tournaments, teams, scrims, and tryouts.
  • To manage your coin balance and shop orders.
  • To send tournament/order confirmations to our Discord server (only tournament name, your username/team, and order details are shared — never your password or private account data).
  • To show leaderboards and stats using your username and game stats.

3. Third-Party Services We Use

  • Supabase — our database and authentication provider. Your account data is stored securely with them.
  • Discord — used only to post tournament/order notifications via webhook. We don't access your Discord account.
  • Adsterra — our ad network for the "Watch Ad" coin-earning feature. Ad networks may use cookies to serve ads; see Adsterra's own privacy policy for details.

4. Data Sharing

We do not sell your personal data. Information is only shared with the third-party services listed above, strictly to operate the app's features (tournaments, orders, ads).

5. Your Choices

  • You can update your username and Free Fire UID anytime from your Profile.
  • You can request account/data deletion by contacting us (see below).
  • Guest/demo mode stores data only in your browser — nothing is sent to our servers.

6. Children's Privacy

BLOOD REIGN is intended for users old enough to play Free Fire under its own age guidelines. We do not knowingly collect data from young children beyond what's needed to run tournaments.

7. Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy as the app grows. Continued use of the app after changes means you accept the updated policy.

8. Contact Us

Questions about this policy or your data? Reach out to us on our Discord server (link in the app footer).
